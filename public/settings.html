Imou accounts

Each account here is an Imou Open Platform app (appId + appSecret). Everything is stored in this browser only (localStorage), unencrypted, and sent to the serverless functions with each request — fine on a machine you trust, not on a shared one. Camera passwords are kept per account, and so is the data-center region.

Account details

Get appId / appSecret from Console → My Apps → App Info. Pick the data center your app actually belongs to (Console → Basic Info → My Info) — the wrong one returns OP1005. Per-camera passwords live on the Cameras page.
